# PaceLeague Support

Email us at [Contact email]. If your question is about your account, write from the address you signed up with and include your runner name.

If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first.

## Common questions

### I forgot my password

Email us from the address you signed up with, and we'll send you a temporary password. Sign in with it, then change it in Profile → Privacy → Change password.

### A run was held for review

A run with unusual GPS, such as a stretch at car speed, is held instead of counted. A person checks held runs, usually within 2 days, and the result shows on the run. GPS glitches happen, so a held run is never treated as proof of cheating. To ask for another look, email us with the run's date.

### A run hasn't synced

Runs are saved on your phone first and upload when you have a connection. Profile → Imports and sync lists every run still waiting and why, with Sync now and Try again. If a run stays stuck, tap Send diagnostics on that screen, then email us.

### My run didn't earn XP

A run earns league XP when it has at least 100 m, at least 1 minute of active time, and GPS for at least 80% of that time. Runs from Apple Health or a watch count the same way when they come with a GPS route. Workouts without a route, runs typed in by hand and imported files stay in your history and count for your weekly goal and streak. Profile → Support & legal → How scoring works has all the rules.

### PaceLeague Pro

In the US, Pro is $4.99 a month, or $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. On iPhone, manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you change phones or reinstall the app, tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases: you can ask for one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Deleting your PaceLeague account doesn't cancel a subscription.

### Export or delete your data

Profile → Privacy → Export my data gives you your data as a JSON file, with a GPX file for each route. Profile → Privacy → Delete account deletes your account. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) says what is kept afterwards, and for how long.

### Someone is bothering me

Open their profile to report or block them. Runs, comments, clubs, group runs and challenges each have their own Report button, and reports go to our moderators. Profile → Blocked runners lists the runners you've blocked, and you can unblock them there.
